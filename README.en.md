STS2 Solver is a combat solver Mod for Slay the Spire 2, single player. When a fight starts it reads the current combat and the real random-number state, searches the card-play line for the whole fight, and plays cards and potions in the game automatically.

In Slay the Spire 2, quitting and re-entering restarts the fight with the same random outcomes; this is the save-and-load (SL) trick, and the solver relies on it to predict each step. Cards and potions are played through the game's own actions.

STS2 Solver's search strategy and position scoring are based on [CombatSolver](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver), with a simulation kernel redesigned for search: the combat state is one compact block of memory, which gives better search performance. In the same time it expands about 13× as many search nodes as CombatSolver, and its search takes about a quarter of the time; together with some scoring adjustments, it plays better (see Performance below). The kernel was checked action by action against the game itself on over a thousand combats generated by the [sts2-sim](https://github.com/iRyougi/sts2-sim) simulator.

It currently supports game v0.111.0 and needs RitsuLib 0.6.0 or later, installed separately.

## Performance

Compared with CombatSolver on the same 105 combats.

Combat results:

| Character | Combats | Wins (STS2 Solver / CombatSolver) | Avg HP lost, combats both won |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Ironclad | 21 | 20 / 20 | 19.6 / 22.9 |
| Silent | 21 | 14 / 14 | 7.3 / 7.9 |
| Defect | 21 | 20 / 18 | 22.7 / 25.1 |
| Necrobinder | 21 | 18 / 18 | 19.9 / 22.4 |
| Regent | 21 | 20 / 17 | 20.6 / 22.1 |
| **Total** | **105** | **92 / 87** | **18.5 / 20.7** |

| Encounter | Combats | Wins (STS2 Solver / CombatSolver) | Avg HP lost, combats both won |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Normal | 40 | 40 / 40 | 11.1 / 12.2 |
| Elite | 40 | 34 / 31 | 23.9 / 24.8 |
| Boss | 25 | 18 / 16 | 26.8 / 33.9 |

STS2 Solver wins every combat CombatSolver wins, plus 5 more. Of the 87 combats both win, STS2 Solver loses less HP in 27, CombatSolver in 9, and 51 are equal.

Search performance:

| | Median time | Mean time | Max time | Nodes expanded per second |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| STS2 Solver | 2.2 s | 3.4 s | 44.9 s | 37,497 |
| CombatSolver | 8.5 s | 13.4 s | 120.4 s | 2,956 |

In the same time STS2 Solver expands 12.7× as many nodes as CombatSolver; compared combat by combat, the median is 14.4×. It searches faster in 93 of the 105 combats, 356 s versus 1404 s in total, about a quarter of the time, and still expands more nodes per combat (median 3.8× as many): it covers a larger search space, which makes better routes easier to find.

Test conditions:

- Combats: 21 randomly generated decks per character (Ascension 10; 8 normal, 8 elite, 5 boss fights), 105 combats in total.
- Play: both solvers on the Medium budget, full auto to the end of the fight. CombatSolver is main after 0.50.1 (`5c773caa`), playing in the real game running headless with Smart potions; STS2 Solver is main (`98b8ee95`), playing in its offline kernel.
- Time and nodes: the search at the start of each combat. Both solvers almost always search only once, then follow that route. Both ran on the same machine.
- Search parallelism: 4 for both.

## Usage

- During a fight the solver panel at the top left shows search progress, the route for the whole fight and the step being executed; you can drag, resize and collapse it.
- Three modes: **Manual** (you press search and execute yourself), **Auto-search** (it searches every turn and you press **Play turn**) and **Full auto** (it searches and plays by itself). The default is Auto-search, and your choice is kept.
- If a result differs from the prediction or an action cannot be performed, execution stops at once; press **Search again** to continue. When Full auto pauses this way, click **Full auto** in the title bar to resume.
- Unsupported content (for example cards added by other Mods) is rejected instead of being played blindly.
- It can be installed together with CombatSolver, but let only one of them execute each turn, or they get in each other's way.
- **Settings** holds play speed, search tier, search parallelism and more; **Potions** sets how each potion is used, and **Growth** sets how much extra HP the solver may spend for each long-term gain outside the fight (for example Feed or Genetic Algorithm).
- The panel follows the game language: Chinese for Simplified or Traditional Chinese, English otherwise.
