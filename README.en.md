STS2 Solver is a combat solver Mod for Slay the Spire 2, for single-player Standard, Dread and Blitz, including the Fool. When a fight starts it reads the current combat and the real random-number state, searches the card-play line for the whole fight, and plays cards and potions in the game automatically.

In Slay the Spire 2, quitting and re-entering restarts the fight with the same random outcomes; this is the save-and-load (SL) trick, and the solver relies on it to predict each step. Cards and potions are played through the game's own actions.

STS2 Solver's search strategy and position scoring are based on [CombatSolver](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver), with a simulation kernel redesigned for search: the combat state is one compact block of memory, which gives better search performance. Search speed and combat results are shown in Performance below. The kernel was checked action by action against the game itself on over a thousand combats generated by the [sts2-sim](https://github.com/iRyougi/sts2-sim) simulator.

## Performance

STS2 Solver **v0.2.7** versus CombatSolver **v0.51.5**, on the same 126 combats in game v0.113.1.

STS2 Solver wins all 102 combats won by CombatSolver, plus 1 more. In the 102 combats both win, STS2 Solver loses 1.01 less HP on average.

**Search performance:**

| | Median time | Mean time | Max time | Nodes expanded per second |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| STS2 Solver v0.2.7 | 1.7 s | 3.0 s | 29.9 s | 22,487 |
| CombatSolver v0.51.5 | 6.0 s | 10.7 s | 120.9 s | 3,049 |

STS2 Solver searches faster in 118 of the 126 combats. Opening searches take 373 seconds in total versus 1,343 seconds for CombatSolver, a 72.2% reduction, with about 7.4× as many nodes expanded per second.

**Test conditions:**

- Standard mode, Ascension 10, all content unlocked, full starting HP. Each of the six characters has 21 randomly generated decks and encounters: 8 normal, 8 elite and 5 boss fights.
- Both solvers execute full combats in the real game running headless, using their respective Medium search settings, 4 search threads and Smart potions. Optional choices at combat setup are skipped for both.

## Installation

1. In your Steam library, right-click Slay the Spire 2, open Properties → Betas, and choose `public-beta` to update the game to v0.113.1.
2. Subscribe to STS2 Solver on the Steam Workshop; Steam offers to subscribe to the required RitsuLib Mod along with it.
3. Start the game. The first time it loads Mods it asks for confirmation; agree, then restart the game.

## Usage

- During a fight the solver panel at the top left shows search progress, the route for the whole fight and the step being executed; you can drag, resize and collapse it.
- **Auto-search** is on by default and searches each turn. **Auto-play** is off by default; enable it to play automatically after a search finishes and continue through later turns. Both switches are saved, and Auto-play requires Auto-search. You can also press **Play turn** or **Play combat** manually. Stopping a search keeps its candidate without playing it; stopping execution switches Auto-play off.
- If a result differs from the prediction, a death is projected or an action cannot be performed, execution stops at once; press **Search again**, or confirm and press **Play combat** to continue.
- Unsupported content (for example cards added by other Mods) is rejected instead of being played blindly.
- It can be installed together with CombatSolver, but let only one of them execute each turn, or they get in each other's way.
- **Settings** holds play speed, search tier, search parallelism and more; **Potions** sets how each potion is used, and **Growth** sets how much extra HP the solver may spend for each long-term gain outside the fight (for example Feed or Genetic Algorithm).
- The panel follows the game language: Chinese for Simplified or Traditional Chinese, English otherwise.
